What We Offer

Everything you need to stay on top of your health

From immediate guidance to long-term health management, our full suite of services has you covered at every stage of your health journey.

Personal Doctor

A dedicated doctor who knows your history and manages your care continuously.

Teleconsultation

Speak with a licensed doctor anytime you need to, by phone or video call.

24/7 Nurse on Call

Immediate clinical advice at any time of day or night. For members only.

Home Visits

Arrange an in-person nurse or doctor visit for yourself or family anywhere in Nigeria.

Care Coordination

Urgent & Emergency
