Brief your AI like a new staff member.

Most bad AI answers come from bad briefs. Here’s the four-part brief we use at Eaglescroft every day.

Part one
Role.

Give it a job title.

Part two
Context.

Give it what a new hire would need to know.

Example“Our customers mostly message on WhatsApp, after work hours, and want a quick price and a visit date.”

03 / 07
Part three
Task.

One clear job, with a finish line.

Example“Write five reply templates for first-time enquiries. Each must end by offering two visit times.”

Part four
Format.

Show it what good looks like.

Example“Under 60 words each. Warm, plain English. No emoji. Return them as a numbered list.”
