## Fri 30: Cockpit Chat: "Ask AI how it fails."
**Graphic:** `design/concepts/3d-set/post-3.png` ✅

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> Don't ask AI if your idea is good. It will usually say yes.
>
> Ask it how your idea fails.
>
> The prompt we use before every big decision at Eaglescroft:
> "Here's my plan: [paste plan]. Act as a sceptical investor. Give me the 3 strongest reasons
> it could fail, and what would prove each one wrong."
>
> Then a human decides. Every time.
> Save this for your next planning meeting.
> #PracticalAI #PromptEngineering #Eaglescroft

## Sun 1: Ground School: "5 questions before you plug in AI"
**Graphic:** `design/concepts/3d-set/post-2.png` ✅

**LinkedIn / Facebook**
> Before you plug AI into your business, ask these 5 questions:
> 1. Who can see our data?
> 2. Is it stored, or used to train the model?
> 3. Is every action logged?
> 4. Who is accountable when it's wrong?
> 5. Can we switch it off tomorrow?
>
> If a vendor can't answer all five clearly, wait.
> Save this for your next software demo.
> #AIGovernance #DataPrivacy #AIForBusiness #Eaglescroft
