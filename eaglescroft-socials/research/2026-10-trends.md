# Trend notes: October 2026

Researched 23 Oct 2026. Most platform sources are marketing blogs and vendor guides, not
first-party data. Treat the numbers as directional and replace them with our own analytics
after the first month.

## Platform signals

### LinkedIn
- **Dwell time, saves and sends** are the signals most commonly cited for ranking. Content people
  stop on and keep wins.
- **Document carousels** still report the highest engagement of any format (~6–7% in vendor
  benchmarks), especially for frameworks and tutorials. A minority view says LinkedIn is shifting
  toward authority and conversation over slide decks. So carousels need real substance, not
  slide-padding.
- **External links** in the post body reduce reach (one blog claims ~60%). Put links in the
  first comment.
- **Early comments** (first hour) matter; engagement bait, pods and polls reportedly lost reach in
  early 2026.
- "How I automated X" / transparent AI-workflow posts are named as a trust-building format.
  That's exactly our Flight Report / Cockpit Chat angle.

### X
- Feeds are ranked by a Grok-based model (code published Jan 2026); predicted engagement and relevance decide reach.
- **Replies that get a reply back** are weighted heavily; mutual relationships surfaced more since Jul 2026.
- **Links in the main post are penalised**: put them in a reply.
- **Threads and native video** are reported to drive the most reach. Dwell/watch time weighted more than before.
- **Posting more doesn't mean more reach.** A per-creator cap limits how often you appear in one follower's feed.
- Mutes and blocks reduce distribution broadly, so stay sharp but not inflammatory.

### Facebook
- Page organic reach is low (~1–5% of followers; small pages do better than large ones).
- **Reels** are the main organic lever. They're pushed to non-followers while standard post reach falls.
- Groups out-engage Pages; replying quickly and asking real questions beats posting volume.
- Treat organic as a test bed and boost what already works.

## Topic signals for our niche

- **From chat to bounded agent workflows.** The conversation has moved from "use ChatGPT to
  draft" to giving AI bounded tasks (lead research, follow-ups, support triage) with humans
  keeping approvals. This maps directly to our lead engine and automation work.
- **Governance and trust** are becoming buying questions: who sees the data, what's logged,
  who's accountable. Good material for Ground School ("what to ask before you plug AI into your business").
- **Someone has to run the agents.** Small firms need a person who maintains prompts,
  permissions and outputs. This is a future-of-work angle with a skills message.
- **Nigeria: adoption figures conflict.**
  - World Bank: AI use among *formal* Nigerian firms rose from <5% (2022) to ~40% (2025), on par with the US.
  - CSEA (528 digitally enabled firms, Lagos/Rivers/Abuja): 74%+ report AI use, but adoption is
    limited among smaller firms.
  - BusinessDay commentary: ~10.1% adoption in Q1 2026; most SMEs have only experimented; **cost**
    is the main barrier.
  - Other barriers cited: limited technical expertise (~65% of SMEs), power, and unreliable internet.
  - **Our angle:** The gap between big firms and small businesses is the opportunity. Cheap,
    practical, WhatsApp-first AI wins are our message.
