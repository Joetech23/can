# Competitor scan: October 2026

First pass, from web research on 24 Oct 2026. It maps who else competes for our audience's
attention and where the gaps are. Sources are mostly directories, vendor blogs and creator
profiles. Follower counts and rankings are approximate, so re-check before quoting any of them.
LinkedIn and X feeds could not be read directly from this environment. Next step: Joshua
saves 5 recent posts from each name below into the Drive inbox so we can study their formats.

## 1. Nigerian digital agencies (direct service competitors)

| Agency | Where seen | What they lead with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wild Fusion (Lagos) | Clutch #1 for PPC, 4.5★ | SEO, social media management, web development |
| Brand Envoy Africa (Lekki) | Clutch digital strategy, 4.8★ | Digital strategy |
| Anakle | Agency roundups | Digital marketing, content |
| Digibutterfly | Agency roundups | Digital marketing |
| Doingsoon | Agency roundups | Digital marketing |

**What we see:** these agencies sell classic digital marketing: SEO, ads, social, websites.
None of them shows up in AI-automation searches. The Nigerian AI conversation is driven by
events (GITEX Nigeria, Africa AI & Agentic Summit) and product companies, not agencies showing
real builds.

**Our gap:** be the Nigerian-rooted, globally serving agency that **shows the AI systems it
builds**: chatbots, CRM automation, lead engines and AI video. Nobody local owns that.

## 2. Global AI-automation agencies and creators (the content benchmark)

| Name | Model | Content engine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Morningside AI / Liam Ottley | Coined the "AI Automation Agency" model; agency plus education | YouTube (~400K), Skool community (250K–300K members); gives knowledge away free |
| Nick Saraev | Make.com automation, AI agency | YouTube (~200K); step-by-step builds |
| Listicle agencies (Botsify, BotPenguin, Beleaf, Media.Monks) | Chatbots, CRM/ERP automation, AI agents | Mostly SEO blogs, little personality |

**What we see:** the winners teach in public and show real builds, then sell. Their audience
is mostly aspiring agency owners in the US and UK, not business owners in emerging markets.

**Our gap:** teach **business owners** (not would-be agency owners) with **African and global
real-world context**: WhatsApp-first customers, power and data costs, mixed-currency clients.

## 3. GoHighLevel ecosystem agencies (CRM / AI Employee)

GHL Scale Up, RSLA and many Fiverr sellers publish on HighLevel's **AI Employee** (Conversation
AI, Voice AI, Reviews AI, Content AI). Reported add-on pricing is $50/month (Growth) or
$97/month (Unlimited) per sub-account, on top of the base plan. Sources disagree on Voice AI
language support (English/Spanish vs 19 languages), so verify in HighLevel's docs.

**Our gap:** honest, practical explainers ("what it actually does, what it costs, who should
skip it") based on audits we've actually done.

## 4. AI educators in Nigeria and Africa (Joshua's personal space)

| Name | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Experience AI (NerdzFactory + Raspberry Pi Foundation) | Teacher training: 1,142 educators in year one; 2026 rollout |
| Emmanuel Akpan, AI Teacha / EduAIHub | AI in African education, research-funded |
| Mojeed Abisiga, AI Every Time / DataGlobal Hub | AI content hub, data and AI community |
| ICEDT pilot (Federal Unity Schools) | Practical AI tools for teachers |

**What we see:** the field is **institutional and academic**: programmes, research and
summits. Very few practitioners show AI running a real business every day.

**Joshua's gap:** the **practitioner's voice**: "here's what I built today, here's how a
trader, a lecturer or a company can use it tomorrow." Daily, plain-language and hands-on.
