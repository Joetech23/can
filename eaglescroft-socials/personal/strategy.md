# Joshua Obaje: personal social strategy

A separate track from the Eaglescroft brand. Eaglescroft says "we" and shows the agency's
systems. Joshua says **"I"** and translates AI for everyday people, every day.

**Approved bios** are in the original handoff: the long bio plus three short ones. Every
profile links to Eaglescroft ("Founder & CTO, Eaglescroft").

## Who he speaks to

| Audience | What they need from Joshua |
| --- | --- |
| **Small traders and market sellers** | Free, phone-first AI wins: WhatsApp replies, price lists, flyers, records. Pidgin-friendly on Facebook |
| **Academics and students** | Using AI for research, writing and teaching **responsibly**: what's fine, what's cheating, how to check outputs |
| **Business owners and professionals** | Practical workflows that save hours: reports, proposals, customer service, hiring |
| **Companies and teams** | Adoption done right: AI policies, training, governance, picking tools |
| **Builders and peers** | Build-in-public: what he shipped, what broke, what's next |

## Pillars

| Series | Share | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Today I built** | 25% | Daily production log: what he worked on, with a screenshot or photo and one lesson (client details removed) |
| **AI for…** | 30% | One audience per post: *AI for traders*, *AI for lecturers*, *AI for small companies* |
| **Tested this week** | 15% | New features and products he actually tried, with an honest verdict |
| **Client wins** | 15% | Anonymised project stories in his own words (sector + region only) |
| **The bigger picture** | 15% | Future of work, Africa's place in AI, careers, opinion |

## Voice

- First person, warm, direct. Short sentences. Sounds like him talking.
- Every post ends with something the reader can **do today**.
- Honest about limits: "AI got this wrong, here's how I caught it."
- Global outlook from an African base: examples from Abuja **and** London, Lagos **and** Sydney.
- Privacy line, stricter than the brand's: no client names, no money figures, no academic or
  agriculture roles, no degree details, no family or location specifics beyond "Abuja".

## Platforms and rhythm

| Platform | Rhythm | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn (personal) | Daily, Mon–Sat | Main platform. Text posts with an image or carousel; reshare one Eaglescroft post per week with his own take |
| X | Daily | Shorter cuts, threads on Tue and Thu, build-in-public replies |
| Facebook / Instagram | 4–5 per week | Trader and community angle; Pidgin-friendly captions where natural |

## Visual identity: "the notebook"

Clearly Joshua's, recognisably linked to Eaglescroft, but not the agency's look:

- **Light, paper-white canvas** (`#F7F8FC`) with a faint ruled-notebook or grid texture, as
  opposed to the brand's deep-navy panels.
- **Navy Sora headlines**, Raleway body and the brand blue accent, so it sits in the same family.
- **Handwritten-style highlights**: a blue marker underline or circle on the key word, drawn as
  SVG strokes.
- **Signature corner**: round avatar plus "Joshua Obaje · Founder, EAGLESCROFT" in place of the
  agency's eagle brand bar.
- **Real-life imagery first**: his desk, screens and phone shots, plus Pexels photos of everyday
  African and global workplaces.
- Formats: quote cards, "my desk today" photo cards, 5–7 slide notebook carousels, screenshot
  annotations.

approved; swap in `<img>` inside `.av`.
