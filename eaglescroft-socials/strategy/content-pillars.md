# Content pillars

Five recurring series. Each has a name so the audience learns the format, and a matching
eyebrow on the graphic, led by the Eaglescroft flight marker (`HANGAR NOTES`, `FLIGHT REPORT` …).

| # | Series | Eyebrow | Share of posts | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Flight Report**: problems solved | `FLIGHT REPORT` | 30% | We get results with AI |
| 2 | **Ground School**: practical AI education | `GROUND SCHOOL` | 30% | We can teach you |
| 3 | **Hangar Notes**: project ideas and build logs | `HANGAR NOTES` | 15% | We build real things |
| 4 | **Cockpit Chat**: conversations with AI at Eaglescroft | `COCKPIT CHAT` | 15% | We know how to work *with* AI |
| 5 | **On the Radar**: trends and the future of work | `ON THE RADAR` | 10% | We see what's coming |

---

## 1. Flight Report: problems solved

A real problem from our work, the AI-assisted approach, and the outcome. Anonymised.

- **Shape:** Problem → What we tried → What worked (and the human part) → Result → Lesson you can steal.
- **Formats:** portrait card or 5–7 slide carousel (LinkedIn), Reel walk-through (Facebook), thread (X).
- **Seed topics from our work:**
  - Turning a raw 13,000-record prospect list into 12,000+ scored leads, each with a tailored outreach hook.
  - Cleaning and migrating messy CRM data for an international client.
  - A WhatsApp + CRM automation that stops a service business losing enquiries after hours.
  - Auditing a GoHighLevel build: what usually breaks and how to spot it.
  - A brand-and-document pipeline that turns a brief into client-ready proposals, decks and reports.
- **Template:** [`templates/flight-report.md`](../templates/flight-report.md)

## 2. Ground School: practical AI education

Teach one useful thing per post. Nigerian/SME context first.

- **Shape:** One idea, one scenario, one action the reader can take today.
- **Formats:** carousel (best on LinkedIn), short video/Reel, single-image tip, X thread.
- **Seed topics:**
  - "Your first AI assistant for your business: a 30-minute setup."
  - Prompting a model like you'd brief a new staff member.
  - 5 everyday tasks to hand to AI this week (and 3 you shouldn't).
  - AI for a WhatsApp-first business: auto-replies, FAQs, order capture.
  - How to check an AI answer before you trust it.
  - Agents vs. chatbots vs. automations, in plain English.
- **Template:** [`templates/ground-school.md`](../templates/ground-school.md)

## 3. Hangar Notes: project ideas and build logs

What's on the bench at Eaglescroft: ideas, prototypes, progress, lessons.

- **Shape:** What we're building → why → this week's progress or obstacle → what's next.
- **Formats:** behind-the-scenes photo/screen recording, short update post, X build-in-public.
- **Seed topics:**
  - The AI video agent that produces product motion videos for SaaS companies.
  - Faceless and documentary-style content made with AI scripting, voice and video tools.
  - Our internal tooling: how Eaglescroft itself runs on AI.
- **Template:** [`templates/hangar-notes.md`](../templates/hangar-notes.md)

## 4. Cockpit Chat: conversations with AI at Eaglescroft

Real exchanges between our team and AI assistants (Claude and others), showing how we think
with AI rather than just asking it things.

- **Shape:** The question we brought → the key turn in the conversation → what changed in our
  work → the prompt pattern you can reuse.
- **Formats:** stylised chat-excerpt graphic (never a raw screenshot), carousel, X thread.
- **Rules:** scrub client context, keep excerpts short, always add our own judgement. The post
  is about the *human + AI* method, not "look what the AI said".
- **Template:** [`templates/cockpit-chat.md`](../templates/cockpit-chat.md)

## 5. On the Radar: trends and the future of work

Short, grounded commentary on AI news and research that matters to our audience.

- **Shape:** What happened → what it means for a Nigerian business or professional → what to do.
- **Formats:** single-image take, text post, X quote-post.
- **Rule:** every stat has a source logged in [`research/`](../research/). Be clear about where
  sources disagree (e.g. Nigerian AI adoption figures range from ~10% to 74% depending on who was surveyed).
- **Template:** [`templates/on-the-radar.md`](../templates/on-the-radar.md)

## Repurposing: one story, three platforms

Every substantial piece (usually a Flight Report or Ground School) is written once and cut three ways:

| LinkedIn | Facebook | X |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carousel PDF + 150–250 word caption | Reel (30–60s) or portrait image + shorter, warmer caption | 5–8 post thread, first post carries the hook + image |
