# Brand voice on social

Source of truth for colours, type and core voice is the Eaglescroft brand system. This file
adapts it for social feeds.

## Positioning

**Niche:** AI education, practical applications and the future of work.

**Promise:** We show you how AI actually gets used inside a working digital agency: real builds,
real problems, real workflows. Then we help you do the same.

**Audience, in priority order**
1. Business owners and founders (Nigeria first, then UK / US / Australia) who know AI matters but
   don't know where to start.
2. Professionals and team leads who want to work faster and stay relevant.
3. Students, NYSC members and early-career people building AI skills for the future of work.
4. Peers: builders, agencies and AI practitioners. They build credibility and collaborations.

## Voice

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Practical: show the workflow, the prompt, the before/after | Hype: "AI will replace everyone", "10x overnight" |
| Plain-spoken: explain it like a smart friend would | Jargon-heavy or academic |
| Honest: include what didn't work and where a human still decides | Selling magic |
| Confident and partner-minded: "we", "you / your business" | Preachy, lecturing, guru tone |
| Locally grounded: Nigerian context (power, data costs, WhatsApp-first customers) | Copy-pasted Silicon Valley takes |

- **Person:** "we" on the Eaglescroft account, "I" on Joshua's account. Address the reader as "you".
- **Spelling:** British-leaning (optimise, colour, organisation).
- **Aviation touch:** use it occasionally (series names, a closing line, a CTA): *lift-off,
  flight plan, ready for takeoff*. Once per post at most. Many posts need none.
- **Headlines on graphics:** sentence case with a full stop, set in **Sora** at the H2 tier (headline
  ~64–76px on a 1080px graphic), never poster-sized. *"Your inbox can answer itself."*
- **The name on its own is EAGLESCROFT.** In brand bars, chat labels, sign-offs and covers, write it
  in capitals. In sentences it stays *Eaglescroft* (*"At Eaglescroft, we…"*).
- **Emoji:** none on graphics or headlines. In captions, at most one or two functional ones
  (an arrow, a pointer), never decorative.
- **Numbers as proof:** compact, with `+` or `%`, e.g. "12,000+ leads scored". Use them only when they
  pass the privacy line below.

## The privacy line

Joshua keeps a low-key, private profile. Every post passes this check before approval:

- [ ] **No client names**, logos, screenshots with client data, or details that identify a client.
      Describe the client by sector and region: *"a UK catering business"*, *"a healthcare membership platform"*.
- [ ] **No money figures**: no fees, revenue, budgets or fundraising amounts.
- [ ] **No personal details** beyond the approved bio: no academic or agriculture roles, no degree details.
- [ ] **Screenshots are scrubbed**: names, phone numbers, emails, API keys and URLs blurred or replaced.
- [ ] **AI chat excerpts are ours**: only conversations from Eaglescroft's own work, with any
      client context removed.
- [ ] **Claims are true**: anything stated as a result actually happened; trend stats carry a
      source in the post or in `research/`.

Eaglescroft, its services and `eaglescroft.com.ng` **can and should** be named and promoted.
